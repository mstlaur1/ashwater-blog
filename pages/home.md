Welcome to the musings of a father, airline pilot, and habitual tinkerer.

This site is my journal: notes on self-hosting, LLMs, aviation, fatherhood, and whatever else I'm building or breaking. It runs on a Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W drawing half a watt, because in an age of bloated frameworks and infinite cloud compute, there's something satisfying about creative constraints.

Just words, served from a shelf in the Laurentians.
